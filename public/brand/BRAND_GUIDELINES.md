# IntelliCare — Master Brand Guidelines

> **Single Source of Truth**: The official IntelliCare brand identity combines clinical trust, mathematical intelligence, and modern human healthcare operations.

---

## 1. Master Logo & Mark

The official IntelliCare symbol is an organic, interlocking 4-petal ribbon cross representing:
- **Vertical Axis**: Patient care, clinical vigilance, and institutional stability.
- **Horizontal Axis**: Forward predictive foresight and balanced resource distribution.
- **Center Overlap**: The continuous synthesis of machine intelligence and human governance ("AI recommends. Humans decide.").

## 3. Brand Rules & Clear Space

- **Aspect Ratio**: Always preserve the 1:1 symbol proportions and standard wordmark kerning.
- **Clear Space**: Maintain a minimum margin equal to 50% of the mark's height around the entire lockup.
- **Backgrounds**: The official logo is optimized for deep dark environments (`#050814` to `#0A1020`). For rare light documents, use `/brand/intellicare-logo-light.svg`.
- **Prohibited Alterations**:
  - ❌ Do NOT replace the symbol with a heartbeat line, medical plus cross, or generic AI star.
  - ❌ Do NOT apply harsh neon drop shadows or oversaturated glow filters.
  - ❌ Do NOT stretch, rotate, distort, or rearrange the symbol elements.
  - ❌ Do NOT render "Care" in pure white or "Intelli" in cyan—the two-tone wordmark is non-negotiable.
