# SOP-STF-02: Nurse-to-Patient Staffing Ratios
Department: Nursing Administration | Owner: Chief Nursing Officer

## Minimum ratios
Intensive Care Unit (ICU): 1 registered nurse to 2 patients (1:2) at all times.
Emergency Department (ED): 1 nurse to 3 patients (1:3) for treatment areas.
General Ward (medical-surgical): 1 nurse to 4 patients (1:4).
These ratios are mandatory minimums. A unit that cannot meet its ratio must escalate to the nursing supervisor within 30 minutes.

## Float pool
The hospital maintains a float pool of cross-trained nurses. Float nurses are assigned first to units breaching ratio, prioritising ICU, then Emergency, then General Ward. A float nurse may only be assigned to ICU if ICU-competency certified.

## Overtime
Overtime may be approved only after the float pool is exhausted. No nurse may exceed 12 consecutive hours or 4 overtime shifts in a 14-day period. Overtime approvals must be recorded by the nursing supervisor.
