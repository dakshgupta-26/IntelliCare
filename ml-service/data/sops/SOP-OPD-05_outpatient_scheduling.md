# SOP-OPD-05: Outpatient Appointment Scheduling and Overbooking
Department: Outpatient Services | Owner: OPD Manager

## Clinic hours and rooms
Outpatient clinics run 09:00 to 17:00. Each consultation requires one doctor and one consultation room. Doctors may not be double-booked.

## Slot length
Slot lengths may be set from predicted consultation duration rather than a fixed length, rounded to 5-minute increments. First visits and patients with multiple chronic conditions typically require longer slots.

## Overbooking
Controlled overbooking is permitted for patients with high predicted no-show risk: the reserved slot may be shortened in proportion to the no-show probability. Overbooking must not be applied to emergency or urgent patients.

## No-show follow-up
Patients flagged as high no-show risk receive an additional reminder call 24 hours before the appointment. No-show predictions must not use protected attributes (gender, age, religion, caste, income) and are reviewed periodically for bias.

## Waiting time target
Patients should be seen within 15 minutes of their appointment time. The OPD manager reviews the daily average and 90th-percentile waiting time.
