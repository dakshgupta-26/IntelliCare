# SOP-EQP-04: Ventilator and Critical Equipment Allocation
Department: Biomedical Engineering and Critical Care

## Pool
Ventilators are managed as a central pool. Allocation priority: ICU first, then Emergency Department resuscitation bays.
Planning assumption: approximately 45% of ICU patients and 8% of Emergency patients may require mechanical ventilation.

## Shortage
If demand exceeds the pool, the critical care lead requests transfer of devices from partner facilities and activates the equipment shortage protocol. Allocation of scarce ventilators between individual patients is a clinical decision made by the treating physicians and is outside the scope of operational decision-support tools.

## Turnaround
Each ventilator requires cleaning and functional checks between patients (approximately 2 hours) before re-deployment.
