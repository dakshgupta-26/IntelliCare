# SOP-EMG-06: Emergency Override of Scheduled Workflows
Department: Emergency Medicine | Owner: Medical Director

## Principle
An emergency patient is never held in an automated queue. Any emergency arrival immediately bypasses appointment queues, predictive buffers and optimization runs.

## Outpatient emergencies
If a patient in the outpatient area requires urgent care, the next available doctor in the relevant department sees the patient immediately after finishing any consultation already in progress. Remaining appointments are rescheduled with minimal disruption, and affected patients are informed of the delay.

## System failure
If the scheduling or optimization system is unavailable, staff revert to deterministic priority rules: emergencies first, then urgent cases, then patients in order of appointment time. Manual override is always available and is recorded in the audit log.
