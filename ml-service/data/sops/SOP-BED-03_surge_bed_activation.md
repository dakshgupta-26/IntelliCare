# SOP-BED-03: Surge Bed Activation
Department: Hospital Operations | Owner: Bed Management Office

## Surge capacity
The hospital holds 30 flexible surge beds that may be opened in the General Ward or Emergency Department when forecast demand exceeds staffed capacity. Surge beds must be opened in blocks with nurse coverage arranged at the unit's minimum ratio before patients are placed.

## Activation criteria
Surge beds are activated when forecast occupancy exceeds 100% of staffed beds within the next 12 hours, or when Emergency Department boarding exceeds 6 patients for more than 2 hours.

## Deactivation
Opened surge beds that remain unused for 8 hours should be closed to avoid idle staffing cost. Deactivation is decided by the bed manager.

## Corridor care
Placing patients in corridors is a last resort and requires notification of the hospital administrator and a documented plan to resolve within 4 hours.
