# SOP-ICU-01: ICU Capacity Escalation
Department: Critical Care | Owner: Director of Critical Care | Review: annual

## Trigger levels
Level 1 (Watch): ICU occupancy at or above 85% of staffed beds, or forecast to reach 85% within 12 hours. The ICU charge nurse notifies the bed manager and reviews step-down candidates.
Level 2 (Alert): ICU occupancy at or above 95%, or forecast to exceed 100% within 24 hours. The operations coordinator may convert up to 6 step-down beds to ICU-capable beds after confirming monitoring equipment and ventilator availability.
Level 3 (Critical): All ICU and converted beds occupied. The hospital administrator must approve diversion of new critical admissions to partner facilities and postponement of elective surgeries requiring post-operative ICU care.

## Step-down transfers
Patients stable for 12 hours without vasoactive support are prioritised for transfer to step-down to free ICU capacity. Transfers must be documented and the receiving ward must confirm nurse coverage before transfer.

## Approval
Converting beds and diverting admissions are recommendations until approved by the hospital administrator or on-call senior physician (human-in-the-loop requirement).
