# SOP-GOV-07: Governance of AI-Assisted Operational Decisions
Department: Hospital Administration | Owner: Chief Operating Officer

## Human-in-the-loop
Recommendations from forecasting, optimization or scheduling systems are advisory. Resource reallocations (bed conversions, staff moves, overtime, equipment transfers) require approval from the hospital administrator or department manager before execution.

## Explainability
Every recommendation must include a plain-language reason and a reference to the relevant hospital policy so that approvers can verify it is policy-compliant.

## Audit
All approvals, rejections and modifications are logged with the approver's identity, time, the original recommendation and any modification.

## Data protection
Operational models use aggregate census data. Patient identifiers used for scheduling are pseudonymised, and all data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
